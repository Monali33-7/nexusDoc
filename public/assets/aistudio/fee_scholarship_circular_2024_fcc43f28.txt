SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Accounts and Student Welfare Circular No. SIT/FEE/2024/012
Date: 20 July 2024
Subject: Fee Payment and Scholarship Guidelines, Academic Year 2024-25

A. Fee payment
1. The tuition fee for each semester must be paid on or before 31 August 2024.
2. A late fee of Rs. 100 per day will be charged after the due date, up to a maximum of Rs. 2000.
3. Fees can be paid online through the college portal or by demand draft in favour of "Principal, SIT Vijayawada".
4. Fees once paid are not refundable, except in case of withdrawal of admission before the start of classes, when 80% is refunded.

B. Scholarships
1. Students whose family annual income is Rs. 2.5 lakh or less are eligible to apply for the government fee reimbursement scholarship.
2. Required documents: income certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card, previous marksheet, and bank passbook copy.
3. The last date to submit scholarship applications to the college office is 30 September 2024.
4. Students must maintain 75% attendance to remain eligible for scholarship renewal.

SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Circular No. SIT/ACAD/2024/031
Date: 10 July 2024
Subject: Revised Attendance Policy for Undergraduate Students (supersedes Circular SIT/ACAD/2022/014)

1. Minimum attendance: Every student must have at least 75% attendance in each semester, calculated across all subjects combined.
2. Condonation: Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation by paying a fee of Rs. 1000. A valid reason such as illness, family emergency, or participation in university-approved events must be supported by documents.
3. Detention: Students with attendance below 65% will be detained and must repeat the semester. No condonation is possible below 65%.
4. Applications for condonation must be submitted through the college portal at least 7 days before the semester examinations.
5. This circular is effective from the academic year 2024-25 and replaces all earlier attendance circulars.

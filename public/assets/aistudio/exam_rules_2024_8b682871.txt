SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Examination Branch Circular No. SIT/EXAM/2024/009
Date: 02 August 2024
Subject: Rules for Semester End Examinations

1. Hall ticket: Students must carry the hall ticket and college ID card to every examination. Entry is not allowed without them.
2. Reporting time: Students must reach the exam hall 20 minutes before the start. Late entry is allowed only up to 30 minutes after the exam begins. After that, entry is denied.
3. Mobile phones, smart watches, and any electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall. Violations are treated as malpractice.
4. Re-evaluation: Students may apply for re-evaluation within 7 days of result declaration by paying Rs. 1500 per subject.
5. Supplementary exams are conducted within 45 days of the declaration of results.
6. Malpractice cases will be referred to the Examination Discipline Committee. Punishment may include cancellation of that semester's exams.

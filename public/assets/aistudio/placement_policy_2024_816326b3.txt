SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Training and Placement Cell Policy
Policy No. SIT/TNP/2024/002
Date: 25 June 2024
Subject: Campus Placement Policy for Final Year Students

1. Eligibility: A student must have a minimum CGPA of 6.5 and no active backlogs to sit for campus placement drives.
2. Registration: Students must register with the Training and Placement Cell portal before the start of the 7th semester.
3. One-offer policy: A student who receives one offer may not apply for other companies, except for a "dream offer", which is an offer with a salary at least twice the first offer.
4. Once a student accepts an offer and later declines it without a valid reason, the student will be debarred from further placement activities.
5. Students must attend at least 80% of the pre-placement training sessions to remain eligible.
6. Dress code: Formal attire is mandatory for all placement drives.

SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Student Welfare Circular No. SIT/HOS/2024/005
Date: 05 August 2024
Subject: Hostel and Library Rules

HOSTEL
1. Hostel gate closing time is 9:30 PM for all students. Late entry requires written permission from the warden.
2. Visitors are allowed only on Sundays between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM, in the visitors' room.
3. Cooking appliances, heaters, and electric irons are not allowed in rooms.
4. Leave from the hostel on weekends requires a signed parent consent form submitted to the warden before 5:00 PM on Friday.
5. Ragging is strictly prohibited and is punishable under the college anti-ragging policy and UGC regulations.

LIBRARY
1. Library hours are 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM on working days and 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. The library is closed on Sundays.
2. Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books for 14 days.
3. A fine of Rs. 2 per day per book is charged for late returns.
4. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at the current price plus a Rs. 100 processing fee.

SAMPLE INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, VIJAYAWADA
Circular No. SIT/ACAD/2022/014
Date: 15 June 2022
Subject: Attendance Policy for Undergraduate Students

1. Minimum attendance: Every student must have at least 75% attendance in each semester, calculated across all subjects combined.
2. Condonation: Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation by paying a fee of Rs. 500 and submitting a medical certificate.
3. Detention: Students with attendance below 65% will be detained and must repeat the semester.
4. Applications for condonation must be submitted to the Head of Department at least 10 days before the semester examinations.
